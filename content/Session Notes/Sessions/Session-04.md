# Session 4 - The Featherwood Run

**Date:** 2026-03-22
**In-game date:** [If tracked]
**Location(s):** [Stormspire](Session%20Notes/Locations/Stormspire.md), outside the city
**NPCs encountered:** [Veil Corryn](Session%20Notes/Characters/Veil-Corryn.md), [Garruk](Session%20Notes/Characters/Garruk.md)

---

## Summary

The party's first mission with new members Halifax Byrd and Ouligo: escort three lumbermen into the woods outside Stormspire to collect featherwood. They fought off awakened trees and a lizardman guardian, collected the featherwood, and returned to the city where they purchased a Lesser Broom of Flying from a magic woodworker in the Crucible District.

## Events

### New Mission: Collect Featherwood

[Veil Corryn](Session%20Notes/Characters/Veil-Corryn.md) assigns the party a new mission to escort a group of three lumbermen into the woods outside the city to collect featherwood. The party now includes two new members: [Halifax Byrd](Session%20Notes/Characters/Halifax-Byrd.md) (human fighter) and [Ouligo](Session%20Notes/Characters/Ouligo.md) (half-goliath paladin).

### Departure

The party is warned that the woods contain powerful magical beasts and fey courts. [Garruk](Session%20Notes/Characters/Garruk.md), a human lumberman, introduces himself before the group departs for the woods on wagons.

### On the Road

During the wagon ride, [Sue Nommy](Session%20Notes/Characters/Sue-Nommy.md) has a casual conversation with the horses about how tasty oats are. [Tolliver](Session%20Notes/Characters/Tolliver-Wist.md) insists that [James](Session%20Notes/Characters/James-Everett.md) give him a gold coin, concentrates on it, claims to see the image of a man in the coin, then pockets it.

### Camp for the Night

The party travels until nightfall and makes camp. Watch order:
1. Tolliver and Ouli
2. Halifax
3. Sue
4. James

Nothing happens during the watches. The party completes a long rest.

### Into the Woods

The next day, the party continues deeper into the woods and comes across a ruined hut. They hear the sound of groaning wood before being ambushed by ents. The three lumbermen flee to the wagon, leaving the party to fight. The party defeats the trees, but a lizardman attacks them for killing his "charges." The party knocks him out and leaves him to awaken after they depart.

### The Featherwood Grove

The party finds a grove of featherwood trees. The lumbermen collect the featherwood, and the party receives some as well.

### Return to Stormspire

The party returns to the city and is directed to the Crucible District to find a magic woodworker. Using the featherwood, the woodworker crafts a **Lesser Broom of Flying** which the party purchases for 100gp.
- Base fly speed: 25 feet
- Over 200lb: speed reduced to 15ft
