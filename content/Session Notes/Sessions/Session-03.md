# Session 3 - The Mad Wizard

## Summary

The party descended deeper into the cult hideout in the Gloam and confronted Malreth Voss, a mad wizard who had been using Alira's power to enhance himself. They defeated him, found the physical chained door from Alira's dreams, sensed a strong power behind it, discovered a complex spell classification system, recovered Alira, and left.

## Events

### Deeper into the Hideout

The party continued exploring the cult hideout after the ambush at the entrance (Session 2).

### Confrontation with Malreth Voss

In a large room filled with books, the party encountered [Malreth Voss](Session%20Notes/Characters/Malreth-Voss.md), a mad wizard. He attacked the party on sight. The party defeated him.

### Finding Alira

[Alira](Session%20Notes/Characters/Alira.md) was found in the room. Malreth Voss had been using her power to enhance himself.

### The Chained Door

In the back of Malreth Voss's room, the party found the chained door -- the same door from the children's dreams. Upon investigating it, the party sensed a strong power behind it. This is likely connected to the Silver Tyrant's imprisonment.

### The Spell Classification System

The party discovered a complex system for deciphering the levels and classes of spells within the hideout.

### Departure

The party left the hideout with Alira.
