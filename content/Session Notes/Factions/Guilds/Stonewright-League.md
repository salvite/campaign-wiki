# Stonewright League

**Type:** Guild (Construction / Mining)
**Leader:** [Thaldrum Stonehand](Session%20Notes/Characters/Thaldrum-Stonehand.md)

## Overview

Construction, mining, architecture, and masonry. Members regard their labor as foundational to the empire -- literally and symbolically.

## Culture

Tradition-bound and meticulous.

## Operations

- Manages quarries, imperial roads, and fortifications
- Constructs defensive walls, bridges, and monuments
- Operates subterranean mining guilds and gemcrafting workshops
- Conducts secretive excavations seeking ancient relics
