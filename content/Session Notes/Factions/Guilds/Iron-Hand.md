# Iron Hand

**Type:** Guild (Labor Union)
**Leader:** [Marik Voss](../../Characters/Marik-Voss.md)
**Base:** [Stormspire](../../Locations/Stormspire.md) -- Common Ring
**Allegiance:** The working class; potentially revolutionary

---

## Overview

Represents dockworkers, miners, farmers, craftsmen, and day laborers -- the working class foundation of the empire. Members view themselves as essential to imperial prosperity and demand proper recognition.

## Elemental Association

No official ties, but Earth and Water spirits receive occasional reverence among members.

## Culture

Tough and fiercely independent. Emblem: a clenched iron fist.

## Operations

- Organizing labor strikes
- Securing fair compensation
- Protecting worker rights
- Mutual aid during economic hardship
- Runs orphanages, soup kitchens, and apprenticeship programs
