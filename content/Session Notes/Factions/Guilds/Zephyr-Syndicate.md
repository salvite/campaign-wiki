# Zephyr Syndicate

**Type:** Guild (Trade / Smuggling / Espionage)
**Leader:** [Sythros Vale](Session%20Notes/Characters/Sythros-Vale.md) -- Gale-King
**Base:** [Stormspire](Session%20Notes/Locations/Stormspire.md) -- Zephyr Heights
**Allegiance:** [Thal'jin](Session%20Notes/Characters/Thaljin.md) (Air element)

---

## Overview

Controls airship trade, courier services, smuggling, and espionage. Operates the fastest message and trade routes in Vaeloris. Manages skyship docks and smuggler routes across imperial borders.

## Culture

Opportunistic, fast-moving, and fiercely independent.

## Operations

- Airship courier and trade services
- Smuggling across imperial borders
- High-altitude duels between skyships
- Intelligence gathering
