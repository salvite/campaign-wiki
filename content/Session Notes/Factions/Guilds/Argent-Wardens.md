# Argent Wardens

**Type:** Guild (Adventuring / Military)
**Leader:** [Varaen Thorne](Session%20Notes/Characters/Varaen-Thorne.md) -- Guildmaster
**Base:** [Stormspire](Session%20Notes/Locations/Stormspire.md) -- Common Ring
**Allegiance:** Ostensibly independent; rumored imperial ties

---

## Overview

Monster hunting, exploration, bodyguard contracts, and frontier defense. Beloved protectors of the empire, giving commoners a path to heroism.

## Culture

Discipline, honor, and strength. Entry requires passing rigorous trials to ensure competence and loyalty.

## Known Facilities

- **The Argent Colleges** -- a research institution in [Stormspire](Session%20Notes/Locations/Stormspire.md), used by the party to research the Silver Tyrant (Session 2)

## Operations

- Guildhalls serve as training academies and taverns
- Local chapters sometimes function as sheriffs or militia in rural areas
- Publish heroic tales in city broadsheets to glorify imperial protection
- Teach orphans reading, writing, and mathematics; promising candidates receive advanced training
