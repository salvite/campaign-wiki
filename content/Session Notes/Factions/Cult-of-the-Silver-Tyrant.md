# Cult of the Silver Tyrant

**Type:** Cult
**Leader:** Unknown
**Base:** The Gloam (underground [Stormspire](Session%20Notes/Locations/Stormspire.md))
**Allegiance:** The Silver Tyrant

---

## Overview

A cult devoted to the Silver Tyrant, the ancient enemy defeated by Emperor [Veylorn Stormcrown](Session%20Notes/Characters/Veylorn-Stormcrown.md) over 800 years ago when the [Stormcrown Empire](Session%20Notes/Factions/Stormcrown-Empire.md) was founded. Identified by [Veil Corryn](Session%20Notes/Characters/Veil-Corryn.md) from their medallion symbol: a door being broken from chains.

## Structure

Unknown. Members encountered so far wear black coats with silver accents, carry sickles, and wear door-and-chains medallions. At least some are capable of magic (firebolt).

## Goals

- **Confirmed:** Seeking to release the Silver Tyrant from imprisonment
- Searching for "children of power" -- magically talented children needed for this purpose
- Kidnapping these children from orphanages, particularly those with prophetic dreams
- The children are subjected to "testing" and "reclassification" -- a structured selection process

## Notes

- The door-and-chains symbol matches Alira's prophetic dreams exactly -- the chained door breaking open
- The "Whisper Man" (black feather cloak, floats) is connected but his exact role is unclear -- recruiter? Leader?
- At least six children taken from the Hollow Hearth alone; Tolliver's childhood experience suggests this has been going on for years at multiple orphanages
- The memory erasure aspect (everyone forgot the boy existed) suggests powerful magic at their disposal
- "Testing" and "reclassification" from Alira's burned folder indicates a systematic selection process
