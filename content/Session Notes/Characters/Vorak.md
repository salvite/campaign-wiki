# Vorak

**Race/Type:** Dao (Earth Genie)
**Role:** Bound Court member -- Lord of Stone
**Affiliation:** [Bound Court](../Factions/Bound-Court.md), [Stonewright League](../Factions/Guilds/Stonewright-League.md)
**Location:** [Stormspire](../Locations/Stormspire.md) -- Stoneward (Earth Quarter)
**Status:** Active

---

## Appearance

Colossal stone entity with craggy granite features and veins of silver, emerald, and obsidian. Eyes glow gold and blink slowly. Head crowned with stone spines resembling stalagmites. Beard resembles layered shale or volcanic glass. Wears heavy stone-carved armor decorated with rune-etched slabs and imperial motifs, accented with gold and bronze threading. The ground trembles beneath his footsteps; he can crack stone with his raised voice.

## Personality

The most grounded and unyielding of the Bound Court -- slow to act, slow to trust, and slow to forget. Considers decisions for days that others rush through in moments. Blunt but fair with minimal patience for deception. Prefers concrete agreements over verbal assurances. Beneath his composure lies suppressed resentment -- his foundational work elevates imperial prestige without corresponding recognition. Politically detached compared to the others.

## Titles

- Lord of Stone
- Pillar of the Empire
- Warden Beneath the World

## Background

Once ruler of his own earth realm before being bound to serve the Stormcrown. Built the empire's physical foundations -- fortresses, roads, mines, tunnels.

## Abilities / Resources

- Oversees the Stonewright League (builders, architects, miners)
- Controls underground roadways, vaults, and defensive tunnels
- Manages all imperial fortifications
- Supervises gem, ore, and mineral extraction
- His influence is quietly immense -- nothing in the empire is built without his blessing
