## Description

A vast continent shaped by ancient wars between dragons, genies, and mortal empires. The Stormcrown Empire dominates the central regions while outer territories resist imperial control. Elemental forces remain potent throughout the land.

## History

- Ancient wars between dragons, genies, and mortal empires shaped the land
- ~800 years ago: The Stormfall -- a seven-day catastrophic tempest
- Veylorn Stormcrown unified warring tribes and founded the empire
- The Bound Court genies were bound to serve, establishing elemental infrastructure
