## Objective

Find the missing child [Alira](../Characters/Alira.md) and investigate the disappearance of six magically talented children from the Hollow Hearth orphanage in Stormspire.

## Background

The Argent Wardens identified Alira as a child with magical promise valuable to the guild. She has gone missing from the orphanage. [Veil Corryn](../Characters/Veil-Corryn.md), second-in-command of the Wardens, assigned this as the introductory mission for the party ([James Everett](../Characters/James-Everett.md), [Tolliver Wist](../Characters/Tolliver-Wist.md), [Sue Nommy](../Characters/Sue-Nommy.md)).

Upon investigating, [Ms. Thornbloom](../Characters/Ms-Thornbloom.md) revealed the situation is worse than expected: six magically talented children have been taken in total. Men claiming to be members of various guilds visit the orphanage, interview children, and then those children vanish from the orphanage in the night without explanation.

## Notes

- Introductory quest for the party
- Why are the Argent Wardens interested in a child's magical promise? Recruitment? Or something else?
- Six missing children, all magically talented -- this is organized and deliberate
- The fake guild members are using the guild system as cover to gain access to the orphanage
- Who are they working for? What do they want with magically talented children?
- The disappearances happen at night after the interviews -- someone is coming back for them, or the children are being lured/compelled to leave
- Alira's folder was partially burned -- deliberate evidence destruction. Someone has access to orphanage records.
- "Failed testing" and "reclassified" implies a structured process -- this is an organization, not random kidnappers
- The "Whisper Man" -- wears a cloak of black feathers, almost floats off the ground, whispers to children at the orphanage
- Alira bragged about being "chosen" -- suggests the children are being seduced or recruited, not just snatched
- Alira's dreams are more specific than Keff described: chains on a door breaking, then the door opening. Prophetic vision? Something being unsealed?
- Her journal shows constellations and astrological symbols -- far beyond what an orphan child should know. Who taught her, or is this innate?
- An anti-scrying sigil was under her bed -- someone deliberately shielded her from magical detection. The Whisper Man? Or someone protecting her?
- The "failed testing" and "reclassified" notes from her burned folder combined with the anti-scrying sigil suggest a sophisticated operation
- The party was ambushed immediately after leaving the orphanage -- three attackers in black coats with silver accents, wielding sickles, at least one capable of firebolt. Someone is watching the orphanage or was tipped off about the investigation.
- The street went quiet before the attack -- the hustle and bustle died down, suggesting either magic or the locals knew to clear out
- Black coats with silver accents and sickles -- a uniform? Could be a faction or cult
- **The medallions match Alira's dreams.** Each attacker wore a medallion depicting a door being broken from chains -- the exact same imagery Alira journaled about and screamed about in her sleep. This is not coincidence. These people and Alira's visions are directly connected.
- [x] Identify the medallion / door-and-chains symbol -- identified by Veil Corryn as a **cult of the Silver Tyrant**
- The floating suggests air magic or some other supernatural nature -- but the Whisper Man is NOT Thal'jin
- **Tolliver's connection:** Tolliver grew up in a different orphanage where a boy had similar dreams to Alira. The boy vanished, and everyone forgot he existed -- memory erasure. This means the pattern of targeting dreaming children predates the Hollow Hearth cases and has been going on for some time.

- **The cult of the Silver Tyrant** is behind the kidnappings. Veil Corryn identified the door-and-chains medallion as their symbol. The Silver Tyrant was defeated by Veylorn 800+ years ago -- if the "chained door" in Alira's dreams is literal, this cult may be trying to free or resurrect the Silver Tyrant.
- The children with prophetic dreams may be connected to or needed for whatever ritual/process would break the Silver Tyrant's imprisonment.
- **Confirmed by Goody Two Shoes:** The cult searches for "children of power" to try to release their patron (the Silver Tyrant). The missing children are being used toward freeing the Silver Tyrant.
