# Stormcrown Empire

**Type:** Nation
**Leader:** [Veylorn Stormcrown](../Characters/Veylorn-Stormcrown.md)
**Base:** [Stormspire](../Locations/Stormspire.md)
**Allegiance:** Self-governing imperial power

---

## Overview

The dominant power in central Vaeloris, founded over 800 years ago by Emperor Veylorn Stormcrown after his war against the Silver Tyrant. Veylorn has ruled continuously ever since -- a single unbroken reign of over eight centuries. Following his victory, he bound four djinn to the Stormcrown and used their power to build the empire. Simultaneously feared, admired, and resented. Maintains significant economic prosperity and stability at the cost of freedom and transparency.

## Heraldry

- **Symbol:** A jagged crown surrounded by lightning bolts
- **Colors:** Deep blue, silver, and storm-gray
- **Motto:** *"Order through Tempest, Peace through Power"*

## Structure

- **Absolute monarch:** Emperor Veylorn Stormcrown
- **Administrative council:** The [Bound Court](Bound-Court.md) (four elemental genie sovereigns)
- **Guilds:** Six major guilds handle day-to-day commerce and labor
- **Military:** Elite Stormguard + conventional forces under Khazir's indirect command

## Goals

- Maintain dominion over central Vaeloris
- Expand influence into outer territories
- Preserve the elemental power structure
- Suppress rebellion and dissent

## Power Base

Three pillars of control:
1. Elemental magical abilities
2. Ancient magical agreements (the Bound Court pacts)
3. Formidable military capabilities
