# Emberforge Consortium

**Type:** Guild (Military-Industrial)
**Leader:** [Zarnak Ironflame](Session%20Notes/session%201/Vaeloris/Characters/Zarnak-Ironflame.md) -- Forge-Master
**Base:** [Stormspire](Session%20Notes/session%201/Vaeloris/Locations/Stormspire.md) -- Crucible District
**Allegiance:** [Khazir](Session%20Notes/session%201/Vaeloris/Characters/Khazir.md) (Fire element)

---

## Overview

Blacksmithing, arms manufacturing, siege engines, and elemental forges. Views war as a catalyst for progress and profit.

## Culture

Pragmatic, martial, and hierarchical.

## Operations

- Controls the Crucible District's forges, weapon-smithing academies, and elemental foundries
- Manufactures elemental-infused weapons and war machines for the military
- Strict control over enchanted metallurgy and alchemical fire
- Hosts gladiator tournaments to display battle creations
