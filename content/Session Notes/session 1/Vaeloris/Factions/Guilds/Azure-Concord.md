## Overview

Controls maritime trade, shipbuilding, banking, and mercantile diplomacy. Influence extends wherever trade flows.

## Culture

Elegant, wealthy, and diplomatic.

## Operations

- Controls the Sapphire Docks
- Manages overseas trade routes
- Operates imperial banking institutions
- Private naval escorts and merchant fleets
- Finances infrastructure and private ventures across the empire
- Uses trade embargoes as political weapons
