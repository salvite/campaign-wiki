# Shortbrook Estate

**Type:** Noble Estate / Moorland
**Region:** Unknown (James's homeland, outside Stormspire)
**Controlled by:** [Lord James Everett](../Characters/James-Everett.md), Baron of Shortbrook
**Population:** Effectively 1 (James alone, plus Indigo)

---

## Description

A small, declining noble estate held by the Everett family for centuries. Set on open moorland dotted with ancient barrows like old scars. The estate includes a main house with at least one turret (housing James's chambers) and a small stable. Once staffed with chamberlains, servants, and hunters, now empty after Lord Henry Everett's spending depleted the family fortune.

### The Barrow of James Everett I

The largest barrow on the estate. Originally the final resting place of Lord James Everett, first of his name, undisturbed for nearly five centuries. Lord Henry Everett modified it approximately seven years before the story begins, adding:

- A **stone bowl on a pedestal** at the entrance to an inner chamber -- accepts blood to unlock the door
- A **runed stone door** with blood-wards keyed to Everett lineage -- fresh construction (maybe a decade old) over far older stonework
- A **hidden rectangular chamber** beyond the door, lit by dim magical lanterns, containing a pedestal with [the Tome](../Lore/The-Tome.md) and an old coffin

The barrow entrance was torn open by djinn searching for the Tome. James collapsed it by dissolving the keystone with universal solvent. Currently sealed under rubble.

## History

- Held by the Everett family for centuries
- Lord James Everett I buried in the largest barrow nearly 500 years ago
- Lord Henry Everett depleted the family fortune on adventures
- Henry modified the ancestral barrow to hide the Tome (~7 years before recovery)
- Djinn tore open the barrow entrance searching for the Tome
- James collapsed the entrance trapping the djinn, who then phased through the rubble
- James recovered the Tome; the djinn were consumed by it
