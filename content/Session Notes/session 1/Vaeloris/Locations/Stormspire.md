# Stormspire

**Type:** Capital City
**Region:** Central Vaeloris
**Controlled by:** [Stormcrown Empire](Stormcrown-Empire.md) / [Veylorn Stormcrown](Veylorn-Stormcrown.md)
**Population:** ~1.5 million (humans, dwarves, elves, orcs, genasi, and others)

---

## Description

A marvel of elemental engineering rising from a vast plateau surrounded by steep cliffs and storm-wracked skies. Polished marble streets, sky-piercing towers, and water canals. Combines beauty with oppression -- citizens enjoy better lives than those in outer kingdoms but at the cost of obedience to an immortal ruler.

Character: Proud, orderly, industrious, with simmering underlying tension.

## Defenses

- Storm Wards deflect sieges and airborne attacks
- Bound elementals stationed throughout the city
- Elite Stormguard troops with elemental sigils
- The Lightning Spire at the palace can strike distant armies

## Religious & Cultural Institutions

- **Temple of the Four Winds:** Official temple venerating elemental forces; subtly promotes the emperor as divine mediator
- **Church of the Eternal Storm:** Dedicated to worship of Emperor Veylorn Stormcrown as a semi-divine figure
- **Church of Foreign Faiths:** Serves foreigners and travelers who worship gods and traditions from outside the empire
- **Overflow Church:** A third church serving the general population when the others are at capacity
- Local ancestor shrines and minor deity worship among common folk
- Annual festivals: Stormfall Day, The Crowning of the Storm
