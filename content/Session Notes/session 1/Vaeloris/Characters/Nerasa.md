# Nerasa

**Race/Type:** Marid (Water Genie)
**Role:** Bound Court member -- Lady of the Deep Mirror
**Affiliation:** [Bound Court](../Factions/Bound-Court.md), [Azure Concord](../Factions/Guilds/Azure-Concord.md)
**Location:** [Stormspire](../Locations/Stormspire.md) -- Sapphire Docks (Water Quarter)
**Status:** Active

---

## Appearance

Tall, ethereal woman whose skin shifts between deep blue, pearl white, and seafoam green. Hair flows like living water, adorned with bioluminescent beads and ancient shell fragments. Clothing shimmers like woven mist and kelp. Eyes resemble polished mother of pearl -- those who meet her gaze see visions of memories, storms, and forgotten dreams.

## Personality

Elegant, enigmatic, and hauntingly serene. Communicates in measured tones with poetic or metaphorical language. Listens intently but rarely speaks without purpose. Not malicious, but regards mortals as transient beings easily swayed. Values stability and subtlety. Treats violence as an absolute last resort. Prefers commerce and diplomacy over warfare.

## Titles

- Lady of the Deep Mirror
- Keeper of the Pearl Covenant
- Warden of the Sapphire Veins

## Background

Once ruled her own water realm before being bound to serve the Stormcrown. Built the empire's trade and naval infrastructure.

## Abilities / Resources

- Oversees the Sapphire Docks and all aquatic trade
- Serves as emissary to coastal and aquatic peoples (merfolk, deepfolk)
- Residence: the Veiled Grotto, said to house dream-pearls containing visions of potential futures
- Controls the city's food supply and much of its wealth

## Symbols

- **Sacred Animal:** Jellyfish (graceful yet lethal)
- **Emblem:** A mirrored wave within a silver circle
- **Weapon:** A coral and star-metal trident (primarily ritual)
