# Khazir

**Race/Type:** Efreeti (Fire Genie)
**Role:** Bound Court member -- Lord of Flame
**Affiliation:** [Bound Court](Bound-Court.md), [Emberforge Consortium](Emberforge-Consortium.md)
**Location:** [Stormspire](Session%20Notes/session%201/Vaeloris/Locations/Stormspire.md) -- Crucible District (Forge Quarter)
**Status:** Active

---

## Appearance

Towering, muscular physique with obsidian-red skin cracked with molten veins emitting orange and gold light. Eyes are twin infernos flickering with barely restrained power. Fiery mane that rises and falls with his emotions. Beard curls upward like tendrils of smoke and cinders. Wears heavy ceremonial blackened armor etched with runic flame patterns that glows from within. The air shimmers with heat around him; his footsteps leave scorched impressions.

## Personality

The most volatile, prideful, and ambitious of the Bound Court. Thrives in conflict and battle. Charismatic yet brutal in leadership. Inspires both fear and admiration. Values strength, results, and meritocracy. Deeply competitive toward the other genies. Secretly resents the emperor's authority and harbors desires for independent conquest.

## Titles

- The Flamebound
- Lord of Cinders
- The Ember-Wrought
- Warlord of the Infernal Crucible

## Background

Once ruler of his own realm before being bound to serve the Stormcrown. Built the empire's military-industrial infrastructure in his own image.

## Abilities / Resources

- Commands the Emberforge Consortium (weapons, armor, war machines)
- Controls enchanted armories, siege weapon laboratories, elemental furnace-cities
- Most military generals serve under his indirect authority
- Oversees the Crucible District -- conscript training, steel shaping, arcane warfare
