**Sneak Attack:** 1d6 (once per turn; requires advantage OR an ally within 5 ft of target)

### Weapon Mastery Notes

- **Whip -- Slow:** On hit, target's speed is reduced by 10 ft until the start of your next turn. Great for keeping distance with reach.
- **Shortsword -- Vex:** On hit, you have advantage on your next attack roll against that creature before the end of your next turn. Sets up guaranteed Sneak Attack.

**Typical combo:** Open with shortsword (Vex) to gain advantage on the next attack, then next turn use the whip at 10 ft reach with advantage for Sneak Attack + Slow. Stay out of melee range.

- **Swap initiative:** When you roll initiative, you can swap your result with one willing ally.

## Level Progression Notes

### Level 2
- **Cunning Action:** Dash, Disengage, or Hide as a bonus action. Core rogue mobility.

### Level 3 -- Thief Subclass (Recommended)
The classic tomb raider archetype:
- **Fast Hands:** Use thieves' tools, Use an Object, or Sleight of Hand as a bonus action. Interact with traps, grab items mid-combat, use the Tome (?).
- **Second-Story Work:** Climbing speed equal to walking speed. Jump distance increases by a number of feet equal to DEX modifier.

### Level 4
- **ASI:** +1 DEX (17 to 18) and +1 CON (13 to 14) -- rounds up both modifiers in one go. DEX 18 puts attacks at +6, AC to 15, and Stealth expertise to +8.
