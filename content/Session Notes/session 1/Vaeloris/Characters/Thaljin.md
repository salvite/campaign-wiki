# Thal'jin

**Race/Type:** Djinni (Air Genie)
**Role:** Bound Court member -- Prince of the Skies
**Affiliation:** [Bound Court](../Factions/Bound-Court.md), [Zephyr Syndicate](../Factions/Guilds/Zephyr-Syndicate.md)
**Location:** [Stormspire](../Locations/Stormspire.md) -- Zephyr Heights (Air Quarter)
**Status:** Active

---

## Appearance

Graceful djinni with deep sky-blue skin, luminous white-blue eyes, and silvery hair that flows like wind. Wears garments woven from cloud-silk and storm thread in silver, blue, and ivory, adorned with floating gold bands inscribed with wind runes. His presence generates constant atmospheric disturbance -- papers flutter, flames flicker, and whispers sound without speech. Occasionally shifts into ephemeral, nearly translucent configurations of wind and light.

## Personality

A mercurial trickster philosopher. Communicates through riddles, half-truths, and poetic turns of phrase. Curious, cunning, and detached. Values information over power or loyalty. Respects cleverness -- admires rivals who outwit him more than those he defeats by force. Questions orders and resents confinement.

## Titles

- Prince of the Skies
- Keeper of Secrets
- Lord of the Zephyr Syndicate

## Background

Once ruled his own air realm before being bound to serve the Stormcrown. Built the empire's intelligence and airship infrastructure.

## Abilities / Resources

- Controls the Zephyr Syndicate (airship couriers, smugglers, messenger guilds)
- Manages weather manipulation for festivals and military sieges
- Runs the Whisperwind Spire -- the imperial intelligence/espionage service
- Oversees encrypted communication systems across the empire
- Manages skyship technology and experimental wind-propulsion systems
