## Background

A child at the Hollow Hearth orphanage (run by [Ms. Thornbloom](Ms-Thornbloom.md)) in the Stoneward district of Stormspire. Identified by the Argent Wardens as having some magical promise valuable to the guild. Went missing, prompting the Wardens to send the party to investigate. Alira is one of six magically talented children who have disappeared from the orphanage. Men claiming to be from various guilds would interview the children, who would then vanish in the night without explanation.

## Abilities / Resources

- Extremely promising magical talent -- her journal contains constellations and astrological symbols indicating advanced aptitude
- Recurring dreams/visions about chains on a door breaking and the door opening

## Notes

- Why did the Argent Wardens specifically flag her magical promise?
- Who or what caused her disappearance?
- Is her magic connected to anything larger (elemental forces, the Bound Court, etc.)?
- One of six victims -- this is a pattern, not an isolated incident
- The fake guild members are targeting magically talented children specifically
- Her orphanage folder was partially burned -- someone tried to destroy her records
- Surviving text from the folder indicates she "failed testing" and was "reclassified" -- testing for what? Reclassified to where?
- The last person to speak with Alira was the "Whisper Man" -- a man in a cloak made of black feathers who appears to almost float off the ground
- Alira was bragging about being "chosen" before she disappeared -- she may have gone willingly
- The Whisper Man visits the orphanage regularly and whispers to the children
- Alira talked in her sleep frequently about a door -- she would scream when it got opened in her dreams
- Her journal (found in a drawer in her room) contains constellations and astrological symbols -- signs of extremely promising magical talent
- The journal also references her dreams in more detail: chains on a door breaking and the door opening
- An anti-scrying sigil was found inscribed underneath her bed -- someone wanted to prevent magical surveillance of her
- The journal and the sigil together suggest someone was cultivating or monitoring her magical development
- **The door-and-chains imagery from her dreams matches medallions worn by attackers who ambushed the party outside the orphanage.** Three assailants in black coats with silver accents, armed with sickles and firebolt, each wearing a medallion depicting a door breaking free from chains. Alira's dreams may be visions connected to this group's goals.
