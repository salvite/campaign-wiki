**Domain:** Monster hunting, exploration, bodyguard contracts, and frontier defense.  

**Public Image:** Beloved protectors of the empire, giving commoners a path to heroism.  

**Leadership:** Guildmaster Varaen Thorne — a retired war hero who outwardly champions freedom but secretly reports to the Whisperwind Spire.  

**Culture:** Discipline, honor, and strength. Entry requires passing rigorous trials to ensure competence and loyalty.  

**Notable Features:**
- Guildhalls double as training academies and taverns.
- Local Wardens sometimes act as sheriffs or militias in rural areas.
- Regularly publishes heroic tales in city broadsheets, glorifying the empire’s protection.
