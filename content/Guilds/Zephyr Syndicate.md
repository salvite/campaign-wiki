**Domain:** Airship trade, courier services, smuggling, and espionage.  

**Elemental Tie:** Air (Thal’jin).  

**Leadership:** Gale-King Sythros Vale — a roguish air genasi who balances legitimate business with criminal enterprise.  

**Culture:** Opportunistic, fast-moving, and fiercely independent.  

**Operations:** Control the skyship docks, courier guilds, and the smuggler routes across the empire’s borders.  

**Notable Features:**

- Operate the fastest message and trade routes in Vaeloris.
- Known for daring smuggling runs and high-altitude duels between skyships.
- Frequently accused (sometimes falsely) of harboring rebels.
