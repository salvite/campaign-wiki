**Domain:** Construction, mining, architecture, and masonry.  

**Elemental Tie:** Earth (Vorak).  

**Leadership:** Thaldrum Stonehand — a proud dwarf who values stability over ambition.  

**Culture:** Tradition-bound and meticulous, viewing their work as the literal and metaphorical foundation of the empire.  

**Operations:** Manage quarries, oversee imperial roads and fortifications, and control the Stoneward district.  

**Notable Features:**

- Build the empire’s defensive walls, bridges, and monuments.
- Operate subterranean mining guilds and gemcrafting workshops.
- Known for secretive excavation projects seeking ancient relics.
