## 🌩️ Overview
- A vast land shaped by ancient wars between dragons, genies, and mortal empires.
- Dominated today by **The [[Vaeloris/Stormcrown Empire]]**, which rules much of the central plains and coasts.
- The outer realms—fractured kingdoms, wild frontiers, and ancient ruins—cling to independence, resisting imperial dominance.
- Elemental forces still stir beneath mountains, seas, and skies, remnants of the continent’s mythic past.

## 🏞️ **Central Vaeloris — The Stormcrown Empire**

- Fertile plains, rolling highlands, and mighty rivers.
- Stormspire, the capital, lies at the empire’s heart.
- The empire’s roads and elemental infrastructure bind the region in prosperity and control.
- Constant storms and elemental phenomena linger, symbols of the emperor’s dominion.

## ❄️ **Northern Vaeloris — The Frostward Peaks**

- Rugged mountain ranges and icy wastes.
- Home to frost giants, highland clans, and ancient dwarven citadels.
- Harsh winters and natural defenses make the empire’s influence weak here.

## 🌳 **Eastern Vaeloris — The Verdant Wilds**

- Dense enchanted forests, misty valleys, and ancient ruins.    
- Populated by **fae courts, rebellious wood elves, beastfolk tribes, and outlaw warbands**.
- A chaotic, dangerous frontier where imperial patrols rarely tread.
- Legends speak of sleeping titans and ancient, primal magic resisting the empire’s elemental order.

## 🌊 **Western Vaeloris — The Sapphire Coast**

- Wealthy **merchant republics**, ancient **elven enclaves**, and prosperous **coastal cities**.
- Trade hubs connect Vaeloris to distant continents and exotic goods.
- While technically outside imperial control, the Stormcrown Empire manipulates trade policies and political factions here.
- Water genasi and marid-blooded nobles influence coastal politics, often aligned with Nerasa’s maritime interests.

## 🏜️ **Southern Vaeloris — The Shattered Sands**

- Scorching deserts, ancient ruins, and nomadic peoples.
- Fabled to be the birthplace of the original genie war that brought the emperor to power.
- Scattered oasis cities act as neutral grounds for intrigue, assassination, and diplomacy.
