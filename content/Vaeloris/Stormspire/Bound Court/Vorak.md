**The Unmoving**
**Title:** _Lord of Stone, Pillar of the Empire, Warden Beneath the World_  
**Element:** Earth  
**Role:** Overseer of imperial fortresses, mines, roads, and subterranean wealth

![[z_assets/Vorak.png]]

---

### 💭 **Personality:**

Vorak is the **most grounded** and **unyielding** of the Bound Court—**slow to act, slow to trust, and slow to forget.** He is deeply proud of his work in shaping the empire’s physical and economic foundations. While not cruel, he values **order, durability, and tradition** above emotion or idealism.

- **Stoic and deliberate**, he considers decisions for days that others rush through in moments.

- **Blunt but fair**, he has little patience for games, and prefers contracts to promises.

- His voice is **deep and resonant**, like shifting boulders or a distant cave echo.

- Though he hides it well, he harbors **resentment** that his labor props up the emperor’s glory without proper recognition.


---

### 🧱 **Appearance:**

- **Form:** A massive humanoid of living stone, with **craggy features**, **rough granite skin**, and **veins of silver, emerald, and obsidian** glimmering beneath the surface.

- **Eyes:** Glowing gold, deep-set and slow-blinking, always observing.

- **Hair & Beard:** A sculpted crown of stone spines, like a natural stalagmite helm, and a beard like layered shale or volcanic glass.

- **Clothing:** Wears a heavy mantle of stone-carved armor adorned with **rune-etched slabs** and **imperial motifs**, with gold and bronze threading over his massive chest.

- **Aura:** The ground near him subtly trembles when he walks, and his voice can crack stone if he raises it.


---

### ⛏️ **Role in the Empire:**

Vorak manages:

- The **[[Vaeloris/Stormspire/Guilds/Stonewright League]]**, the empire’s builders, architects, and miners.

- The **underground roadways**, vaults, and defensive tunnels that connect the empire’s fortresses and borders.

- The **imperial fortifications**, ensuring they can withstand siege, storm, or time itself.

- The harvesting of **gems, ore, and raw minerals** from the deep places of [[Vaeloris/Vaeloris]].


He is less politically involved than his peers, but his influence is quietly **immense**, for nothing in the empire is built without his blessing.
