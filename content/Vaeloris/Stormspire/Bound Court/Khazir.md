**The Flamebound**
**Title:** _Lord of Cinders, The Ember-Wrought, Warlord of the Infernal Crucible_  
**Element:** Fire  
**Role:** Master of the empire’s forges, weapons, and military industrial might

![[z_assets/Khazir.png]]

---

### 🔥 **Personality:**

Khazir is the most **volatile**, **prideful**, and **ambitious** of the Bound Court. He thrives in battle, revels in conflict, and considers the empire’s expansion and martial dominance a reflection of his own genius. He is not reckless, however—his **discipline is forged like steel**, his anger carefully honed.

- **Charismatic in a brutal way**, he inspires both fear and admiration.

- **Deeply competitive**, he views the other genies as rivals to be bested, not peers to befriend.

- He respects **strength and results**—meritocracy above all else.

- Beneath the bluster, he secretly chafes under the emperor’s control, dreaming of conquest on his own terms.


---

### 🔥 **Appearance:**

- **Form:** Towering and muscular, with **obsidian-red skin** marbled with molten cracks glowing orange and gold.

- **Eyes:** Twin infernos, flickering with barely restrained power and fury.

- **Hair & Beard:** A roaring mane of fire that rises and falls with his emotions; his beard curls upward like tendrils of smoke and cinders.

- **Clothing:** Wears heavy, ceremonial blackened armor etched with **runic flame patterns**, glowing from within as though it were a forge.

- **Aura:** The air around him shimmers with heat, and his footsteps leave scorched prints behind him.


---

### ⚔️ **Role in the Empire:**

Khazir commands:

- The **Emberforge Consortium**, makers of weapons, armor, and war machines.

- The **military-industrial complex**, including enchanted armories, siege weapon labs, and elemental furnace-cities.

- The **Crucible District**, where conscript training, steel shaping, and arcane warfare all converge.


He is the architect of the empire’s **military dominance**, and most generals serve under his indirect authority.
