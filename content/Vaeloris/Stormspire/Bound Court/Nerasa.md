**The Tidelord**
**Title:** _Lady of the Deep Mirror, Keeper of the Pearl Covenant, Warden of the Sapphire Veins_  
**Element:** Water  
**Role in the Bound Court:** Overseer of commerce, dreams, memory, and the subtle tides of emotion and influence across the Stormcrown Empire.

![[z_assets/Nerasa.png]]

---

## 🌊 **Personality:**

Nerasa is **elegant, enigmatic, and hauntingly serene**—a being of **slow tides and hidden depths**. She speaks in **measured tones**, her words often poetic or laden with metaphor. Of all the Bound Court, she is the most **attuned to the undercurrents**—social, emotional, and magical.

- **Wise and observant**, she rarely interrupts but always listens.

- While not cruel, she sees **mortals as fleeting ripples**, easily disturbed by deeper forces they do not understand.

- She prides herself on **stability and subtlety**—violence is a last resort, and only when all other flows have failed.




---

## 🌊 **Appearance:**

- **Form:** Nerasa appears as a tall, ethereal woman with **skin the color of moonlit surf**—shifting hues of **deep blue, pearl white, and seafoam green**.

- Her hair flows endlessly around her like **living water**, studded with **bioluminescent beads** and fragments of ancient shells.

- Her garments shimmer like **woven mist and kelp**, clinging and drifting as if submerged.

- Her eyes are vast and **reflective like polished mother of pearl**, revealing swirling images of memories, storms, and forgotten dreams to those who dare meet her gaze.

---

## 🌊 **Domain and Influence:**

- Nerasa governs the **Sapphire Docks** and **aquatic trade** throughout the empire.

- She also serves as the **emissary to coastal and aquatic peoples**, including merfolk, sea elves, and deepfolk.

- Her palace wing in the Stormcrown is a **flooded sanctum** known as **The Veiled Grotto**, said to house dream-pearls containing visions of possible futures.




---

## 🌊 **Symbols & Icons:**

- **Sacred Animal:** The jellyfish — graceful, untouchable, and potentially lethal.

- **Emblem:** A mirrored wave inside a silver circle.

- **Weapon (if any):** A **trident made of coral and star-metal**, used more for ritual than war.
