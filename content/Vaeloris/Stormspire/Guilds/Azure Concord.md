**Domain:** Maritime trade, shipbuilding, banking, and mercantile diplomacy.  

**Elemental Tie:** Water (Nerasa).  

**Leadership:** Matron Ilyra Moondrift — an influential water genasi who plays merchants and nobles like chess pieces.  

**Culture:** Elegant, wealthy, and diplomatic. Their influence flows wherever trade flows.  

**Operations:** Control the Sapphire Docks, overseas trade, and the empire’s banking houses.  
**Notable Features:**

- Maintain private naval escorts and merchant fleets.
- Finance both public infrastructure and private enterprises across the empire.
- Use trade embargos as political weapons.
