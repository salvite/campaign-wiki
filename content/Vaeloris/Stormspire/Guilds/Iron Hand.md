**Domain:** Dockworkers, miners, farmers, craftsmen, and day laborers.  

**Elemental Tie:** None officially, though Earth and Water spirits are sometimes revered in their ranks.  

**Leadership:** **Marik Voss**, a bold, charismatic former soldier turned worker-champion.  

**Culture:** Tough, proud, and fiercely independent. They view themselves as the foundation of the empire’s prosperity and demand to be treated as such.  

**Operations:** Organize strikes, secure fair wages, defend workers’ rights, and provide mutual aid during hard times.  

**Notable Features:**

- Their rallies fill city streets with chants and banners bearing the emblem of a clenched iron fist.
- Some radical factions within the Hand quietly seek to overthrow the imperial system.
- The Iron Hand funds orphanages, soup kitchens, and trade apprenticeships for the poor.

**Reputation:** Seen by the common people as protectors, by the elites as dangerous agitators. They walk a fine line between civic force and rebellious movement.
