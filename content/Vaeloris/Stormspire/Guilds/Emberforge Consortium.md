**Domain:** Blacksmithing, arms manufacturing, siege engines, and elemental forges.  

**Elemental Tie:** Fire (Khazir).  

**Leadership:** Forge-Master Zarnak Ironflame — a temperamental fire genasi who dreams of endless conquest.  

**Culture:** Pragmatic, martial, and hierarchical. They view war as a catalyst for progress and profit.  

**Operations:** Control the Crucible District’s forges, weapon-smithing academies, and elemental foundries.  

**Notable Features:**
- Produce elemental-infused weapons and war machines for the legions.
- Maintain strict control over enchanted metallurgy and alchemical fire.
- Host gladiator tournaments showcasing their finest battle creations.
