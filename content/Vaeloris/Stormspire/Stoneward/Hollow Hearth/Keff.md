A small halfling child that [Tolliver Whist](Pcs/Tolliver%20Whist.md) befriended after Keff showed him his hiding spot.
The hiding spot was a hole that Keff could easily fit into and padded with some blankets and rags to keep him from getting dirty. After covering the hole with a patch of grass, Keff becomes nearly impossible to find.
