Though once rulers of their own elemental realms, the Bound Court now serves the [[Tempest Crown]]. Each built part of the empire in their own image, shaping its prosperity—and each now wrestles with their pride, ambition, and resentment over being subordinates to the seemingly immortal [[Vaeloris/Stormspire/Veylorn Stormcrown]].

## 🔥 **[[Vaeloris/Stormspire/Khazir]], the Flame-Tongued (Efreeti of Fire)**

### _Title: Lord of Flame, Master of Forge and Fury_

- **Personality:** Passionate, boastful, and short-tempered. Khazir is proud of his forges, foundries, and battle magics that power the empire’s industry and army. He values bold action over cautious planning and despises stagnation.

- **Role in the Empire:** Oversees the imperial forges, elemental weaponry, arcane war colleges, and the manufacture of siege engines. His smith-priests revere the creative fire of invention.

- **Resentment:** Hates being forced to temper his ambition for the empire's “stability.” He dreams of spreading imperial fire beyond its borders.



- **Appearance:** A towering figure of molten bronze and flame, wreathed in smoke and sparks.


---

## 🌊 **[[Vaeloris/Stormspire/Nerasa]], the Deep Current (Marid of Water)**

### _Title: Mistress of Tides, Queen of the Sapphire Coast_

- **Personality:** Serene, cryptic, and calculating. Nerasa takes pride in the empire’s flourishing ports, aqueducts, and merchant fleets. She speaks in poetic riddles and prefers indirect influence.

- **Role in the Empire:** Controls waterworks, irrigation, naval forces, and merchant guilds. The empire's prosperity flows from her mastery over trade and agriculture.

- **Resentment:** Tired of the empire's warlike tendencies, which disrupt trade and pollute her rivers. Wishes for a golden age of commerce and diplomacy.



- **Appearance:** A graceful, blue-skinned sovereign clothed in flowing silk and surrounded by spiraling currents of water.


---

## 🌪️ **[[Vaeloris/Stormspire/Thal'jin]], the Whispering Gale (Djinni of Air)**

### _Title: Prince of the Skies, Keeper of Secrets_

- **Personality:** Witty, aloof, and curious. Thal’jin delights in freedom and movement, managing the empire’s communication, espionage networks, and airship fleets. He constantly questions orders and plays mind games.

- **Role in the Empire:** Oversees airship travel, messenger networks, and the elite spy service known as the Whisperwinds. Controls weather patterns across imperial lands.

- **Resentment:** Feels trapped by his oath. Hates being confined to mortal schedules and petty politics.



- **Appearance:** A swirling form of crackling wind and faint lightning, appearing either as a robed noble or a shifting breeze.


---

## 🪨 **[[Vaeloris/Stormspire/Vorak]], the Unmoving (Dao of Earth)**

### _Title: Lord of Stone, Pillar of the Empire_

- **Personality:** Stoic, stubborn, and prideful. Vorak built the empire’s fortifications, mines, and roads. He values stability above all else and views his work as the foundation of civilization itself.

- **Role in the Empire:** Oversees construction, mining operations, and defensive works. His architects and stone-priests are everywhere, and the empire’s wealth flows from his quarries and mines.    

- **Resentment:** Resents how the [[Vaeloris/Stormspire/Veylorn Stormcrown\|king]] reaps the glory for his labor. Would rather rule from his mountain halls and be worshiped as the true builder of the empire.



- **Appearance:** A hulking, rock-skinned figure with veins of gold and emerald, radiating the weight of mountains.
